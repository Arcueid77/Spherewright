# 0.5.1 — Native wind recovery

[English](#english) · [中文](#中文)

## English

- Recover individual wind turbines through ordinary native dismantling with exact building/inc conservation.
- Preview generation loss, projected power networks, affected consumers and readiness conditions before executable preparation.
- Verify surviving world data, expected power connectivity and unrelated networks, then independently prepare normal rebuilding.
- Preserve legacy request defaults, original-action idempotency and owned-save continuity.

Windows x64, DSP0.10.35.29151, BepInEx5.4.17. Source and payload hashes are in `spherewright-manifest.json`. [Release notes](https://github.com/Arcueid77/Spherewright/blob/v0.5.1/docs/release-notes/v0.5.1.md)

## 中文

- 通过普通原生拆除回收单座风机，精确核对建筑物品与增产点。
- 执行准备前查看发电损失、预计电网、受影响消费者及未满足条件。
- 核对其他世界对象、预计电网连接和其他电网，再独立准备普通重建。
- 保留旧请求默认值、原动作幂等性及owned存档连续性。

支持Windows x64、DSP0.10.35.29151、BepInEx5.4.17。源码和载荷哈希位于 `spherewright-manifest.json`。[发行说明](https://github.com/Arcueid77/Spherewright/blob/v0.5.1/docs/release-notes/v0.5.1.zh-CN.md)
