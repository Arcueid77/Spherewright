## English

- Bounded multi-planet production, power, logistics and research summaries; native 600-tick measurements, runtime theoretical capacity, utilization and root-cause classification.
- Same-/cross-planet upstream tracing, privacy-allowlisted Overseer bundles and protected per-save logistics-progress windows.
- Runtime-recipe Foundry material drafts with shared-input demand, machine/base-power budgets, explicit external supplies/byproducts and non-executable site/dependency previews.
- Finite native blueprint construction/cancel/fresh continuation with per-object progress, ordinary materials/drones and explicit transport/cost/dependency composition.
- Supported same-family manufacturing-assembler and Mk.I→Mk.II basic-sorter upgrades. Belt/advanced stacking-sorter upgrades are outside the subset.
- Governor pre-execution declarations and continuous game-tick observations; no autonomous expansion.
- Durable Journal-bound tickets and exact owned-primary migration pairs, including `29131 → 29145` v6 disclosure. Reauthorization requires later explicit confirmation and durable ticket consumption before loading; normal Journal-bound default resume consumes after native adoption and confirmed continuity. Compatibility maintenance adds only expired-primary `29145 → 29151`, its fifth durable transition and proved completed-history retention; source-specific runtime evidence remains separate from frozen production acceptance.

Historical production-acceptance source is `2a7a98d1932c404e3a7abc2c11233d9c0b8780d9`, native `0.10.35.29145`. Local three-stage construction with genuine mid-plan recovery, final automatic supplies/kit, both frozen package-layout installation/runtime/recovery checks and a same-session 36060-tick joint-supply window passed their declared scopes. Historical blueprint/Governor results remain separate proofs. See the [production validation](https://github.com/Arcueid77/Spherewright/blob/main/docs/validation/v0.4.0.md) and [native compatibility reports](https://github.com/Arcueid77/Spherewright/blob/main/docs/validation/native-29151-preflight.md); those results apply only to their cited source and scope.

Separate Mod Manager profiles, another computer, installation transaction-crash recovery and a true stationary-carrier-600-tick positive live branch remain unverified. Dark Fog/combat, multiplayer/Nebula and broad third-party compatibility are outside validation; actual interstellar flight/logistics is future work.

- 基于运行时配方的 Foundry 材料草案，覆盖共享输入需求、设备/基础功率预算、明确外供/副产物和不可执行场地/依赖预览。

独立 Mod Manager profile、另一台计算机、安装事务崩溃恢复及真正 stationary-carrier-600-tick 实机阳性分支仍未验证。黑雾战斗、多人/Nebula 和广泛第三方兼容不在验收范围；实际跨恒星航行及物流属于后续工作。
